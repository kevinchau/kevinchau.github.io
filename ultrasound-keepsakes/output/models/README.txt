ILLUSTRATIVE AI KEEPSAKE MODELS

Illustrative AI concept generated from an artistically imagined image. Not a reconstruction of fetal anatomy, not an identity likeness, not an age progression prediction, and not suitable for diagnosis or measurement. Hidden surfaces are inferred by a generic object model. Units are arbitrary.

prenatal.glb / age-one.glb: colored, Y-up meshes for interactive viewing.
OBJ exports include vertex colors; software support for those colors varies.
STL exports contain geometry only. They are NOT print-ready or dimensioned.
Watertightness does not validate self-intersections, minimum thickness,
physical scale, fabrication safety, or slicer behavior.

Generated locally using the official MIT-licensed TripoSR model:
https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSR
The source inputs are AI-generated clay illustrations, not scan volumes.
The age-one model is an independent imagined child, not the same child's
predicted appearance. See ../pipeline-report.json for provenance and QA,
and ../../scripts/generate_meshes.py for the reproducible workflow.
